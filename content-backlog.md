### Pain-point section headline

### Pain-point cards

Use these six cards as the working copy set:

1. **Tool access without operating expectations**  
   Teams get access to models and copilots, but no shared standard for what useful AI work should actually produce.

2. **Pre-AI workflows absorb the gains**  
   New model capacity gets buried under old approvals, handoffs, reporting loops, and delivery structure.

3. **Leadership is too far from the work**  
   AI direction weakens when decision-makers are not close enough to the tools to understand real capability, limits, and tradeoffs.

4. **Shared context is too thin**  
   People and agents cannot do trusted work when the systems, documents, and decisions that matter are only partially accessible.

5. **Output rises while ownership drops**  
   Teams generate more documents, summaries, and drafts, but too much of it adds cleanup, ambiguity, and review load instead of progress.

6. **Cost gets blamed before the operating model**  
   AI spend gets questioned before expectations, process design, context access, and quality controls are fixed.
